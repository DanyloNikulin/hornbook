Turn your language lessons into a journal you can keep, search and practise.

Hornbook brings lesson notes, vocabulary, grammar and review exercises together in one place. Organize separate language pairs, revisit an explanation from class, or practise words with flashcards and spaced repetition.

Build your own learning library
• Keep structured lesson notes with examples, vocabulary and grammar.
• Search across lessons and browse a shared glossary.
• Review flashcards, take lesson quizzes and consult grammar sheets.
• Add lessons by hand, import JSON, or turn transcripts and recordings into study material with optional AI tools.

Your journal stays in your folders
Lessons are stored locally as JSON and Markdown. You can read and edit existing lessons offline, copy the journal for backup, and choose where it lives. No Hornbook account is required. Fictional Spanish and Italian demo lessons help you explore the app.

Choose how AI processing works
Optional local transcription and lesson extraction use separately downloaded tools and models, including Whisper and Ollama. Downloads can be large, and performance depends on your computer and selected model. You can also connect a supported cloud provider using your own account or API key. Cloud processing sends the required material to that provider and may incur its charges. AI-generated lessons should be reviewed for accuracy.

Hornbook is free and open source. The interface supports English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Dutch, Portuguese, Swedish and Ukrainian.
