Hornbook Privacy Policy
Effective date: 13 September 2026
Publisher: Danylo Nikulin

Hornbook is a language-learning journal that stores your materials on your computer. No Hornbook account is required. The app does not include advertising or built-in analytics and does not automatically send your journal to the publisher.

Local data
Hornbook stores lessons, vocabulary, review progress, transcripts, imported audio/video and images, preferences, and processing files or logs in your journal folder and local application folders. Materials you import may contain personal information. You choose which materials to add and process.

Optional AI processing
Reading and editing existing lessons does not require a cloud AI service. If you configure local transcription and a local language model, processing takes place on your computer. If you choose a hosted provider or a remote endpoint, Hornbook sends the input needed for that operation to that destination. This may include recordings for transcription, transcripts, prompts, lesson text and selected images for extraction. Supported integrations include OpenAI, Anthropic, compatible API endpoints and separately configured coding assistants. Those services apply their own privacy policies and retention rules. Use only material you have permission to process and review your selected provider before sending personal information.

Connections and downloads
Optional tools and models require network downloads from their distributors, such as GitHub, Hugging Face or model registries used by Ollama. These services receive ordinary connection information, including your IP address. Microsoft handles Store delivery and updates under its own policies. Opening support or source-code links connects to the relevant website.

API keys
Connection settings and API keys entered in Hornbook are saved locally in your journal's secrets.json file as plain text. Keys are used to authenticate requests to your configured providers. Protect access to your computer and journal folder. Do not include secrets.json in shared journals or public backups.

Retention and control
You can edit and delete your local files and clear configured connections. Deleted or replaced journal items may remain in the journal's _trash folder until you empty it. Processing files, logs, backups and files outside the app installation may remain after uninstalling Hornbook. Remove unwanted copies from your journal and local app folders separately. Any cloud backup or folder synchronization you configure is governed by that service. For data already sent to an external AI provider, use that provider's privacy controls.

Contact and changes
For privacy questions, contact the publisher through https://github.com/DanyloNikulin/hornbook/issues. Issues are public: do not post recordings, private lessons, API keys or other sensitive information. Changes to this policy will be identified by an updated effective date.
